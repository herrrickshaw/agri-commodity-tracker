# GOBARdhan Bulk-Sale of Fermented Organic Manure — Companies, Contacts & Procedure

**Single consolidated reference.** Compiled 2026-07-27 from primary-source Gazette
notifications (Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / DA&FW) held in this
folder, plus the NISG state-coordinator contact roster.

Scope: manufacturers of **Fermented Organic Manure (FOM)** and **Liquid Fermented
Organic Manure (LFOM)** — by-products of GOBARdhan biogas / CBG plants —
authorised to **sell in bulk / loose directly to farmers** under **clause 22(c) of
the Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) Control Order, 1985 (FCO)**.

---

## 1. Procedure — how a plant gets bulk-sale authorisation

**Legal basis**
- **FCO 1985, clause 22(c)** — empowers the Central Government to authorise named
  manufacturers to sell fertiliser in bulk directly to farmers.
- **FCO (Fourth Amendment) Order, 2023** (in force **17 May 2023**) — added a
  proviso exempting FOM/LFOM manufacturers from the **clause 8(3)** authorisation-
  letter requirement for three years, and brought FOM/LFOM (co-products of
  BG/CBG plants) into the FCO fold.

**Step-by-step**
1. **Register the plant** on the GOBARdhan Unified Registration Portal
   (`gobardhan.sbm.gov.in` / `gobardhan.eil.co.in`), operated by DDWS, Ministry of
   Jal Shakti. Portal partner: **EIL (Engineers India Ltd)**; field validation:
   **NISG** state coordinators (§3).
2. **Meet FCO specifications** for FOM/LFOM (product testing / quality conformity).
3. **Apply to DA&FW (Fert Law division)** for bulk-sale authorisation under clause
   22(c).
4. **DA&FW issues a Gazette notification** naming the manufacturer + plant address,
   authorising **bulk/loose sale directly to farmers, valid three years** from
   Gazette publication. These are the seven notifications in §2.
5. **Market Development Assistance (MDA)** — separately, the **Department of
   Fertilizers** grants **₹1,500/MT** on sale of FOM/LFOM/PROM from registered
   BG/CBG plants; claimed through that department, not DA&FW.

**Authorising authority (signatories on the notifications)**
- File reference: **F. No. 1-17/2023 Fert Law** (V uses F. No. 1-17/2025).
- Joint Secretary signing: **Yogita Rana** (Notifications I–II, 2023–24);
  **Franklin L. Khobung** (Notifications IV–VII, 2025–26).
