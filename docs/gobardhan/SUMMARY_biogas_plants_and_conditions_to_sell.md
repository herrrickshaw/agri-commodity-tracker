# GOBARdhan Biogas Plants & Conditions to Sell Bio-Fertiliser — Summary

A one-page synthesis of the authorised biogas/CBG plants and the rules they must
satisfy to sell fermented organic manure (bio-fertiliser). Detail and primary
sources live alongside this file in `docs/gobardhan/` (7 Gazette notification
PDFs, the full companies/contacts/procedure doc, the IFMS selling SOP, and the
HS-3101 trade data).

---

## Part A — The biogas plants

**What they make:** Fermented Organic Manure (**FOM**), Liquid FOM (**LFOM**) and
Phosphate-Rich Organic Manure (**PROM**) — the organic by-products of biogas /
Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG) plants under the GOBARdhan initiative.

**How many:** **119 plant authorisations** to sell in bulk directly to farmers,
issued across **7 DA&FW Gazette notifications** between **Nov 2023 and Feb 2026**.

## Part B — Conditions to sell the bio-fertiliser

To sell FOM/LFOM in bulk and earn the subsidy, a plant must satisfy all of the
following. They fall into four gates.

### 1. Legal eligibility
- **Registered** on the GOBARdhan Unified Registration Portal
  (`gobardhan.sbm.gov.in`).
- Product complies with the **Fertiliser (Control) Order, 1985 (FCO)**. The
  **FCO Fourth Amendment, 2023** (17 May 2023) waived the clause 8(3)
  authorisation-letter requirement for FOM/LFOM for three years.
- The specific plant is **named in a DA&FW Gazette notification** authorising
  bulk/loose direct-to-farmer sale under **FCO clause 22(c)** — valid **three
  years** from publication. (Without this, only packed sale via a marketer is
  allowed.)

### 2. Product quality
- Each batch meets FCO specifications for FOM/LFOM.
- Every batch carries a **Quality Certificate from an NABL / State laboratory**.
- The **batch number on the sale must match the batch number on the QC
  certificate** — enforced at data entry.

### 3. Operational conditions (recorded in the IFMS portal, `dbtfert.nic.in`)
- **Installed capacity & batch size declared** — batch size **< 2000 MT**.
- **MRP entered before any dispatch** — no MRP, no dispatch.
- **Opening balance** declared once; **production** logged per batch.
- Sale routed through one of the permitted channels:
  - **Sale to a Marketer** (fertiliser marketing company), or
  - **Bulk direct** — *Stock Transfer (Self)* or *Sale to Other Retailers*.

### 4. Bulk-sale logistics (per consignment)
- Upload **three proofs**: NABL/State-lab QC certificate, **weighbridge
  receipt**, and **photo of the loaded truck's backside**.
- Record transport: vehicle no., **vehicle capacity < 40 MT**, transport company,
  driver name & mobile; plus invoice, destination state/district and retailer.

### Subsidy (MDA) — the payoff and its conditions
- **Market Development Assistance = ₹1,500 per MT** on FOM/LFOM/PROM sold from a
  registered BG/CBG plant (Department of Fertilizers).
- Requires **State verification**: State officer issues **Proforma B1-MDA**
  (quantity certificate) and **B2-MDA** (quality certificate); any batch that
  fails the State's sample check is carved out as **not eligible for subsidy**.
- Final **DBT claim** is generated and **e-Signed** on the (Old) IFMS billing
  module.

**In one line:** *Register on GOBARdhan → be named in a clause 22(c) bulk-sale
notification → keep each batch NABL-certified with matching batch numbers →
record capacity/MRP/production/sale in IFMS with weighbridge + truck photo and a
<40 MT vehicle → pass State B1/B2 verification → e-Sign the DBT bill to draw
₹1,500/MT.*
