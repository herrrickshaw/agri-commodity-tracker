# GOBARdhan — bulk-sale notifications for Fermented Organic Manure

Gazette notifications by the **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
(Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / DA&FW)** authorising GOBARdhan
biogas/CBG plants to sell **Fermented Organic Manure (FOM)** and **Liquid
Fermented Organic Manure (LFOM)** in **bulk / loose form directly to farmers**,
under **clause 22(c)** of the *Fertiliser (Inorganic, Organic or Mixed) Control
Order, 1985* (FCO). Each authorisation runs three years from Gazette
publication.

## Background

- The enabling change was the **FCO Fourth Amendment Order, 2023** (in force
  17 May 2023), which exempted FOM/LFOM manufacturers from the clause 8(3)
  authorisation-letter requirement for three years — letting GOBARdhan-plant
  manure enter the fertiliser market.
- The bulk-sale notifications above then permitted **loose/bulk** sale (not just
  packed bags), each tranche adding more authorised plants.
- Separately, the **Department of Fertilizers** runs the **Market Development
  Assistance (MDA)** scheme: ₹1,500/MT on sale of FOM/LFOM/PROM from registered
  BG/CBG plants.
